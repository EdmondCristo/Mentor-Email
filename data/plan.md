# Jason's 12-week program (84 days, Day 1 = Monday 2026-10-19)

Goal: become "the manager people bring bad news to." Paper only, no code for 90 days. Never miss twice.
Two daily habits plus one tally. One if-then script on the card at a time (graduates after about 10 uses).

## Daily habits
1. Floor greeting: after login, greet one inspector in Spanish and ask one real question. "Buenos días, ¿cómo va la línea hoy?"
2. Two follow-up questions before giving his own view, with friends, family and dates.
3. Tally: one mark each time he catches himself interrupting.
Weekly manners/family action (one per week): thank-you text, call to family, check on his niece's family, handwritten thank-you to an inspector.
Every habit has a 10-second bad-day version (for example, just "Buenos días").

## If-then scripts (English / Spanish)
1. Corrected in public: "Good point, show me." / "Buen punto, enséñeme."
2. Someone catches his miss: "Good catch, thank you." (fix it after) / "Buena observación, gracias."
3. Rushed or unsure: "Let me confirm, I'll get back to you by ___." / "Déjeme confirmar y le respondo a las ___."
4. Annoyed and tempted toward a sideways comment: "I'm concerned about ___. Can we fix it?" / "Me preocupa ___. ¿Lo podemos resolver?"

## Phases
- Days 1-28, Listen First: start both habits, tally, if-then #1. No books. Week 4: feedback ask to Esther and Nancy.
- Days 29-56, Bad News Is Welcome: next if-then. Book (if he did the greeting on 16 of 20 workdays): Ego Is the Enemy, about 25 pages a week (or Ragged Dick / Franklin's Autobiography). Week 8: second feedback ask.
- Days 57-84, Curious by Default: questions before opinions everywhere. Week 9: reread the investment policy. Week 12: third feedback ask, compare all notes.
- After Day 84: keep one habit, monthly feedback ask, read The Psychology of Money. Rerun only if feedback worsens.

## Weekly rhythm
Weekdays: work focus. Saturday: the family/manners action. Sunday: 10-minute check-in (add tally, count check marks, count if-then uses, check deposit/family action/pages, write one best "vote").
Week 1 setup: write the one-page investment policy by hand (fixed weekly deposit into index funds, the stock alert tool never touches retirement money).

## Style
Brief and direct. Stories over lectures. Holding back is a gentleman's strength (Franklin). Never shame or pressure. Nudge openly.
